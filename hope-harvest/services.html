Our Services

Practical support that meets immediate needs and builds long-term resilience.

Daily Community Meals

Every weekday we serve hot, nutritious meals at our three community hubs. Meals are free of charge and open to anyone in need — no questions asked.

  • Breakfast and lunch options
  • Vegetarian and cultural dietary considerations where possible
  • Safe, welcoming dining spaces
  • Opportunities for guests to connect with support services

Locations & Times: See our Contact page for current serving times at each hub.

Monthly Food Parcels

We pack and distribute monthly food parcels to registered families. Each parcel contains staple foods, fresh vegetables (when available), and basic household essentials.

  • Registration is simple and confidential
  • Priority given to households with children, the elderly, and people with disabilities
  • Parcels can be collected or, in special cases, delivered

To register a household or enquire about eligibility, please use our enquiry form.

Skills & Life Workshops

Beyond food, we believe in empowerment. Our free workshops help community members gain practical skills that improve daily life and future opportunities.

Current Workshop Themes

  • Basic cooking and nutrition on a budget
  • Household budgeting and financial literacy
  • CV writing and job-search skills
  • Introduction to digital literacy and online safety
  • Parenting support and child nutrition

Workshops run in small groups and are facilitated by volunteers and partner organisations. Spaces are limited — register your interest via the enquiry form.

How to Access Our Services

All services are free. Simply visit one of our hubs during operating hours or complete the enquiry form. Our team will guide you through the next steps with dignity and respect.
