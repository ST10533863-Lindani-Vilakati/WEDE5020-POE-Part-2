About Hope Harvest

Our story, our people, and the values that guide us every day.

Our History

Hope Harvest Community Kitchen began in 2018 when a small group of neighbours in the Brixton area of Johannesburg noticed that many families in their community were struggling to put food on the table. What started as a simple weekend soup kitchen in a church hall quickly grew as word spread and more volunteers joined.

By 2020 we had expanded to three community hubs and began offering structured food-parcel programmes and life-skills workshops. Today we serve more than 1 200 families every month and continue to grow with the support of local businesses, churches and individual donors.

Our Mission

“Nourishing bodies and building dignity through community support.”

We provide free, nutritious meals and practical support that restores hope and opens pathways to greater self-reliance.

Our Vision

A Johannesburg where no one goes to bed hungry and every person has access to the basic skills needed to thrive.

Our Core Values

  • Dignity – Every person we serve is treated with respect and care.
  • Community – We believe real change happens when neighbours support one another.
  • Transparency – We are open about how donations and resources are used.
  • Empowerment – We aim to equip people with skills, not just temporary relief.
