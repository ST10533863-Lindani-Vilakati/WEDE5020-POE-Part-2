Welcome to Hope Harvest

Founded in 2018 by a group of passionate community volunteers, Hope Harvest Community Kitchen exists to ensure that no one in our city goes to bed hungry. We believe that a warm meal and a listening ear can restore dignity and open doors to better opportunities.

Whether you need support, want to volunteer your time, or wish to partner with us as a sponsor, you are welcome here.
